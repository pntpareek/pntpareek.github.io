Research Overview

Passive Glassy Dynamics & Timescales

Demonstrated that caging controls the mean whereas dynamical heterogeneity (DH) governs the distribution of particle displacements. Analyzed non-Gaussian distributions, overlap functions, and Fickian yet non-Gaussian characteristics.

Relaxation Dynamics in Kinetically Constrained Models

Investigated the crossover in overlap functions from stretched exponential at short times to power-law at long times in spin models governed by energy conservation and domain wall excitations.

Active Glasses & Tunable Fragility

Studied dense assemblies of active particles exhibiting reentrance and tunable fragility (sub-Arrhenius to super-Arrhenius), characterizing the critical glass-liquid surface and active glass lines.
