Research Highlights

Passive Glassy Dynamics & Timescales

Demonstrated that caging controls the mean whereas dynamical heterogeneity (DH) governs the displacement distributions, explaining Fickian yet non-Gaussian behaviour.

Kinetically Constrained Models

Revealed the mechanisms driving crossovers from stretched exponential to power-law decay in conserved energy 1D spin models via domain wall excitations.

Active Glasses & Fragility

Characterized the morphology of critical glass-liquid surfaces, revealing mechanisms behind reentrance and tunable fragility in biological and soft assemblies.
